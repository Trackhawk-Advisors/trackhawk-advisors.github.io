Carvana Exchanges Unsecured Notes to Cut Debt

$4.2bn Exchange Offering of Senior Secured Notes

August 2023

Download PDF

  • Carvana (NYSE: CVNA) launched an exchange offer and cash tender offer on 8/2/23, under a Jul-23 support agreement with its largest creditors; the exchange settled on 9/1/23
  • Unsecured notes were swapped for cash in the case of the 5.625% notes due 2025 ($850 per $1,000)
    • New Senior Secured Notes were collateralized by Carvana and ADESA assets
  • Holders of 96.4% of $5.5bn of unsecured notes participated, receiving $4.2bn of the $4.3bn of secured notes offered
  • Interest starts as PIK and steps to cash: the 2028s pay 12% PIK in year 1, then 9% cash / 12% PIK; the 2030s pay 13% PIK, then 9% cash / 13% PIK; the 2031s pay 14% PIK for two years; all pay 9% cash thereafter
  • The founders bought $126mm of stock and Carvana sold $225mm through an at-the-market program, meeting the $350mm minimum equity raise
  • The exchange cut total debt by over $1.3bn and cash interest by over $455mm a year for two years; S&P raised Carvana to CCC+ and Moody’s to Caa3 after settlement
