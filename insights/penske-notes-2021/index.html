Penske Refinances Senior Sub Notes at Lower Coupon

$500mm Offering of Senior Sub Notes

May 2021

Download PDF

  • Penske Automotive Group, Inc. (NYSE: PAG) priced $500mm of 3.75% 8yr Senior Sub Notes due 2029 on 5/25/21; the notes settled on 6/15/21
  • The proceeds funded the redemption of its $500mm 5.5% Senior Sub Notes due May-26, saving the company ~$9mm of annual interest expense; in the interim, PAG repaid amounts under its US credit agreement and floor plan debt
  • The Notes are subordinated in right of payment to PAG’s senior debt, including its US credit agreement and floor plan facilities, and effectively subordinated to secured debt to the extent of the collateral
