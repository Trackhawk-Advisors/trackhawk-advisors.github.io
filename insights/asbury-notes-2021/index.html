Asbury Funds Larry H. Miller Acquisition with Bonds

$1.4bn Offering of Senior Notes

November 2021

Download PDF

  • On 11/4/21, Asbury Automotive Group, Inc. (NYSE: ABG) priced a $1.4bn, two-part bond offering, finalized as $800mm of 4.625% Senior Notes due 2029 and $600mm of 5.000% Senior Notes due 2032
  • The offering was initially pitched at a total of $1.5bn across both maturities but was trimmed after Asbury boosted the size of a concurrent common stock offering
  • Both tranches priced at the tight end of guidance, 12.5 bps inside talk of 4.75% (2029s) and 5.125% (2032s)
  • Proceeds, together with the common stock sale, additional borrowings and cash on hand, funded the $3.2bn acquisition of Larry H. Miller Dealerships and Landcar Agency (including ~$740mm of real estate), replacing a $2.3bn high-yield bridge commitment
  • Remaining proceeds were used for general corporate purposes, including other dealership acquisitions or capital investments
