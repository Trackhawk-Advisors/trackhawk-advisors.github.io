Gee Automotive’s Acquisition of Tuttle-Click

On 4/28/26, Gee Automotive (Gee) closed the acquisition of the Jim Click Automotive Team and Tuttle-Click Automotive Group (Tuttle-Click)

April 2026

Download PDF

Deal Summary

  • On 4/28/26, Gee Automotive (Gee) closed the acquisition of the Jim Click Automotive Team and Tuttle-Click Automotive Group (Tuttle-Click)
    • Funded with $300mm of 7.25% notes, pushing leverage to ~5.0x
  • Tuttle-Click Highlights
    • 15 dealerships and 3 collision centers
      • 9 dealerships and 2 collision centers in Southern AZ
      • 6 dealerships and 1 collision center in Southern CA
  • Pro Forma Combined Scale
    • 75 franchises across 57 rooftops in 5 states (WA, OR, ID, CA, AZ)
    • 5 collision centers in 3 states (CA, AZ, OR)
  • Ownership Details
    • Gee: founded by George Gee in 1983; majority owned by Bridger Holdings, a West Coast-based investment firm
    • Tuttle-Click: co-owned by cousins Bob Tuttle and Jim Click Jr.; traces to Holmes Tuttle (Bob’s father)
      • Both families fully exited ownership; Gee is keeping the store names and existing management

Sources: Publicly available information including S&P ratings report, press releases, and CreditSights

$300mm 5yr Senior Unsecured Notes

  • Notes Issuance
    • Gee funded the acquisition of Tuttle-Click with its inaugural bond offering, alongside a syndicate of lenders and an equity contribution
    • On 2/9/26, Gee priced a $300mm 5yr senior unsecured notes offering with a 7.25% coupon
  • Credit Profile
    • Gee has a corporate rating of B+/B1 from S&P and Moody’s; its debut senior notes offering has a rating of B+/B3
    • S&P forecasts ~5x leverage, $3.8bn in pro forma revenue, and a 4-4.5% adjusted EBITDA margin
    • Gee’s 5yr maturity is the shortest tenor of any auto retail bonds outstanding
  • Business Profile
    • Gee has acquired 38 dealerships since 2012
