Morgan Add-on Funds South Motors Acquisition

$350mm Add-on to 2031 Senior Notes for M&A

January 2024

Download PDF

  • On 1/8/24, Morgan Automotive Group priced a $350mm add-on to its 8.25% senior notes due Aug-31 at 102.5% of par, a 7.804% yield to maturity
  • The proceeds of the offering, combined with borrowings under the issuer’s revolving credit facilities and cash on hand, funded the nearly $800mm acquisition of South Motors
  • The deal was upsized by $50mm while in market, based on strong investor demand
  • Morgan Automotive is a private U.S. automotive retailer rated BB- / Ba3 at the issuer level (stable outlook), with the notes rated B+ / B2
  • S&P views the tack-on offering as leverage neutral and projects that, pro forma for the acquisition, the company will have $700mm of total liquidity via balance sheet cash, revolver availability, and its asset-based loan facility; Moody’s expects pro forma debt/EBITDA of ~3.1x
  • The initial $500mm 8.25% 2031 Unsecured Notes priced in Jul-23; the notes are first callable on 8/1/26 at $104.125
