Group 1 Taps the Bond Market to Repay Revolver

$500mm Offering of Senior Unsecured Notes

July 2024

Download PDF

  • On 7/25/24, Group 1 Automotive (NYSE: GPI) priced a Rule 144A private offering of $500mm in aggregate principal amount of its 6.375% Senior Unsecured Notes due Jan-30
  • The notes priced at par, 12.5bps inside talk of 6.50% area; they became callable on 7/15/26 at 103.19
  • Net proceeds were used to repay borrowings under its revolving credit facility and for general corporate purposes; GPI had spent ~$690mm acquiring nine U.S. dealerships and other assets in 1H24
  • GPI’s existing debt includes $750mm of 4% Senior Unsecured Notes due 8/15/28. GPI initially printed $550mm of the bonds at par in Aug-20 and returned in Oct-21 with a $200mm tack-on at 100.25, to yield 3.96%
