Group 1 Funds Hennessy Acquisition with Bonds

$1.25bn Offering of Senior Unsecured Notes for M&A

September 2026

Download PDF

  • On 9/8/26, Group 1 Automotive (NYSE: GPI) priced $1.25bn of Senior Unsecured Notes at par in two equal tranches; the 2035 notes carried a 37.5bps higher coupon than the 2032 notes for three additional years of maturity
  • Proceeds, with cash on hand, funded the ~$1.3bn acquisition of Hennessy Automobile Companies (10 dealerships and a collision center in greater Atlanta), announced 7/30/26 and completed 10/19/26
  • Pending closing, net proceeds repaid borrowings under the revolver’s acquisition line, to be reborrowed at closing; the 2032 notes carried a special mandatory redemption at par had the acquisition not closed by 1/6/27
  • Hennessy generated LTM 3/31/26 revenue of $1.7bn and Adj. EBITDA of $124mm; pro forma LTM 6/30/26 Adj. EBITDA is $1,039mm, with net leverage of 4.2x and net secured leverage of 1.8x
  • The notes settled on 9/22/26 and carry the same bond ratings as GPI’s existing senior unsecured notes
