Lithia Taps the Bond Market

Lithia’s $600mm Offering of Senior Unsecured Notes

September 2025

Download PDF

Lithia’s Bond Offering

  • On 9/3/25, Lithia priced $600mm of 5yr, 5.5% Senior Unsecured Notes at par, which was upsized by $100mm while in-market
  • The deal was launched at $500mm and a yield in the high-5%, but strong market demand allowed Lithia to upsize the deal to $600mm while also lowering the coupon to 5.50%
  • The proceeds of the offering were used to pay down revolver borrowings
  • The agencies affirmed LAD’s issuer ratings of BB+ / Ba1 and unsecured debt ratings BB+ / Ba2, all with stable outlooks

Synopsis

  • Lithia’s 5.5% coupon was the lowest yield on a callable non-investment grade bond since Apr-22, and one of only two 2025 non-investment grade bonds to price at 5.5% or lower
  • Lithia was one of two dealer groups to issue bonds in 2025 after non-investment grade market conditions recovered from the Apr-25 tariff-driven volatility
    • AutoNation was the other dealer group to issue in 2025: $600mm of 4-year senior unsecured notes due 2029 at 4.45% and $500mm of 10-year notes due 2035 at 5.89%
  • AutoNation is investment grade (BBB- / Baa3) and Lithia is non-investment grade (BB+ / Ba1); Lithia’s 5.50% 5-year coupon priced between AutoNation’s 4.45% 4-year and 5.89% 10-year coupons, higher than the shorter-dated bond given its lower ratings and lower than the 10-year bond given its shorter maturity, with the market markedly improved from 1Q25
