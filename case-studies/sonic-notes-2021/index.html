Sonic Funds RFJ Acquisition with Bonds

Two-Part Offering of Senior Unsecured Notes

October 2021

Download PDF

  • On 10/15/21, Sonic Automotive (NYSE: SAH) priced a two-part offering of Senior Unsecured Notes comprising $650mm of 4.625% 8yr notes and $500mm of 4.875% 10yr notes; the offering closed on 10/27/21
  • With strong investor demand, SAH upsized the 8yr Notes by $150mm and priced both tranches at the tight end of guidance
  • Proceeds funded the RFJ Auto Partners acquisition and the redemption of SAH’s 6.125% Senior Sub Notes due 2027
  • On 9/22/21, SAH announced its acquisition of RFJ Auto Partners, adding 33 rooftops with LTM 6/30/21 revenue and Adj. EBITDA of $3.2bn and $118.5mm, respectively
