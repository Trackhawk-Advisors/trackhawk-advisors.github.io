Carvana Taps Institutional Loan Market to Repay Bonds

$1.7bn 7yr Senior Secured Term Loan B

August 2026

Download PDF

  • On 8/12/26, Carvana (NYSE: CVNA) priced a $1.7bn 7yr Senior Secured Term Loan B at SOFR + 225bps and 99.75; launched at $1.0bn with talk of S+225–250 at 99.0–99.5, the loan was upsized by $660mm and priced at the high end
  • Proceeds and cash on hand redeem in full the $1.7bn of 9.00% Cash / PIK Senior Secured Notes due 2030 (issued at $1.5bn in Sep-23; the balance includes PIK accretion), with $1.0bn redeemed 8/15/26 and the balance 8/22/26
  • Carvana expects the refinancing to cut cash interest by ~$45mm annually
  • The retired notes came out of the Sep-23 distressed exchange that recapitalized Carvana – this replaces that structure's near-dated tranche at ~275bps lower cash cost
  • The loan was marketed with 2Q26 TTM Adj. EBITDA of $2.6bn and net debt of $2.6bn, or 1.0x net leverage
  • Carvana is now rated BB- by S&P and Fitch (facility BB / BB+) and B2 by Moody's, up from CCC+ / Caa3 after the 2023 exchange
  • The facility amortizes 0.25% quarterly, carries no financial covenant, and is callable at par subject to a 1.00% soft call for six months
  • $2.3bn of 9.00% Senior Secured Notes due 2031 remain outstanding as the next refinancing candidate
