Penske’s Take-Private Offer

The Deal: Penske Corporation and Mitsui & Co. Agree to Take PAG Private at $212/share, an 8.6% Premium

Who Are Penske Automotive and Mitsui

  • Penske Automotive: One of the largest auto retailers in the world, with 362 franchised dealerships at 6/30/26: 146 in the US and 216 outside the US, principally in the UK
  • Mitsui & Co., Ltd.: One of Japan’s largest trading houses; a strategic partner to PAG since 2001, providing capital, global logistics networks and operational support

Stated Rationale

  • The bidders believe PAG is better positioned as a private company to manage disruptive technologies, establish its own warranty business and avoid the burdens of public ownership
  • The bidders state the offer gives public shareholders immediate liquidity and certainty of value at a premium
  • Mitsui plans to support PAG’s growth through collaborative projects drawing on its global network
  • Penske Corporation (41.1%), PAG (28.9%) and Mitsui (30.0%) already co-own Penske Transportation Solutions, the truck leasing business

Why This Deal Is Interesting

  • Public franchised dealers trade 27–50% below estimated private-market value; PAG traded 3% above it on 4/29/26, before the proposal
  • If discount to private-market value drove take-privates, SAH, AN and GPI, which trade at the widest discounts, would be the next candidates
  • At $212 per share, the bidders pay an implied blue-sky multiple of ~8.0x vs. a 6.0x private-market assumption

What Bidders Are Paying For

  • Full ownership for a fraction of the price: Penske Corporation and Mitsui already own ~72%; buying the rest of the $13.9bn equity at $212/share costs ~$3.9bn
  • More than a dealer group: Penske Transportation Solutions, commercial truck dealerships and distribution generated ~30% of PAG’s pre-tax income in 1H26
