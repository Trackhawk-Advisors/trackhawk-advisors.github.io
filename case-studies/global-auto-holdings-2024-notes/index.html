Global Auto Taps Bond Market to Fund KWB Acquisition

$540mm Senior Unsecured Notes for M&A

November 2024

Download PDF

  • On 11/14/24, Global Auto Holdings (GAH) placed a $540mm tranche of 4.8yr 11.50% Senior Unsecured Notes, upsized from $500mm
  • The proceeds funded a portion of the cash consideration for the ~$1.2bn (DKK 7.9bn) acquisition of K.W. Bruun (KWB), an auto importer and distributor in Scandinavia
  • GAH is one of the largest global auto retailers, operating 179 franchised dealerships across eight countries, including the US, Canada, and the UK
  • S&P rated the notes at B+ and revised its outlook to negative, as the acquisition points to pro forma adjusted leverage rising more than a turn, to roughly 6x. S&P cited the risk of GAH’s leverage ratio running above 5.5x in 2025 and beyond
  • Moody's rated the new notes at B3, alongside a downgrade to existing senior debt, which reflects an expected material increase in the amount of secured debt, including a $550mm senior secured term loan in connection with the acquisition
  • Global Auto previously tapped the bond markets in Jan-24, when it priced a $1.05bn, two-part offering of Senior Notes to refinance debt from its Oct-23 acquisition of Lookers
