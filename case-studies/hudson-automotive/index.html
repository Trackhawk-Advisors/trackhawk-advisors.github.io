Hudson Upsizes Notes Offering to Refinance Debt

$675mm 8yr Senior Unsecured Notes

May 2024

Download PDF

  • On 5/7/24, Hudson Automotive Group priced $675mm of 8yr 8.00% Senior Unsecured Notes at par, which were upsized in-market from $600mm
  • The deal was initially launched at $600mm with price talk of 8.25%, but strong market demand allowed Hudson to upsize the offering to $675mm while also lowering the coupon to 8.00%
  • The proceeds of the offering combined with proceeds from a new syndicated credit facility will be used to refinance ~$1.4bn of existing debt and purchase $44mm of real estate from Redwood Holdings
  • The bonds were marketed with 2023 Adj. EBITDA of ~$300mm and approximately 2.4x secured leverage and 4.7x total leverage
  • S&P Global Ratings expects leverage to remain around 4.5x over the next few years as margins normalize and the steady pace of acquisitions continues
  • Moody’s forecasts pro forma debt to EBITDA will improve to around 4.25x as a full year of acquisitions is realized and cost discipline is maintained
